I procrastinated so I did the whole project in a 3hr session at 1:00am, and I'm writing this after I woke up ans sleeping. 

Originally I accidentally used a 0201 capacitors when i meant to use 0603, and when uploading my gerbers to JLCPCB the first time I forgot to generate the drill files. But that's all fixed now :D

I used JST connectors for the sole reason that I think they look cool, is it the most practical? Probably not but very nice looking. The hole in the top right is to put it on some string and then onto a keychain, and the chess silkscreen is there because I love to play chess, really cool game imo. I plan to plug this into my laptop through a USB-C to USB-C cable, I usually carry one in my bag. (at the time of writing this only one of the USB-C ports work so I need to fix that at some point)

<img width="797" height="866" alt="Screenshot_2026-08-27_03-42-06" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4cd32510-66c8-4724-b3e1-82392d2b668f" />

I used the CH340C because its cheap and built the rest of the board around it, I also thought it would be cool to be able to switch between 5V and 3.3 Volts and that's why there is a switch on it. Hopefully it works, I'm still not great at PCB design yet so I'm still learning a lot.
